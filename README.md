# Project BluePrint

Project BluePrint is an all-in-one digital library and community hub designed specifically for engineering students. By integrating a web platform and a mobile application, it addresses common academic challenges such as the loss of study material between semesters and the difficulty of finding reliable, curriculum-specific notes.

## 🚀 Features

* **Curriculum-Based Organization:** Study materials are structured precisely according to departmental hierarchy and specific year-wise subjects.


* **Peer-to-Peer Learning:** A dedicated community space where students can collaborate, ask questions, and share hands-on support.


* **Expert Guidance:** Faculty and subject experts can provide structured academic guidance and upload verified study materials.


* **Feedback & Review System:** Ensures the quality of user-uploaded notes and diagrams through community reviews.


* **Mobile-First Accessibility:** Syncs seamlessly between web and mobile devices to support modern learning habits.



## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend (Web):** ReactJS, Tailwind CSS.


* **Mobile App:** React Native.


* **Backend:** Node.js or Python FastAPI.


* **Database:** SQL, Firebase, or MongoDB.


* **Storage:** Cloud-based storage to efficiently handle large file uploads without compromising app speed.



## 🎯 Target Audience

* **Primary Users:** 1st to 4th-year undergraduate engineering students across fields like Computer, IT, AI & DS, Mechanical, Civil, and Electrical.


* **Secondary Users:** Senior students and alumni interested in mentoring juniors and building a professional reputation.


* **Contributors:** Subject experts and faculty providing verified resources.



## ⚖️ Legal & Community Guidelines

* **Data Privacy:** Adheres to data protection laws for user account and data security.


* **Copyright & Ownership:** Student creators retain the copyright of their original notes, while the platform maintains a licensing clause to host the works.


* **Prohibited Material:** Uploading scanned versions of whole paid textbooks is strictly forbidden. Accounts may be terminated for repeated intellectual property infringements.



## 🗺️ Implementation Phases

1. **Research & UI:** Finalize topics for 1st and 2nd-year departments and design UI/UX.


2. **Backend Foundation:** Configure the server, database, authentication, and topic structures.


3. **Core Features:** Build file viewing, search/filter functionality, and community spaces.


4. **User Contributions:** Develop secure upload systems and review mechanisms for quality control.


5. **Mobile Integration:** Sync the React Native app with the backend and ensure file viewing responsiveness.


6. **Testing & Launch:** Conduct beta testing with a small group of 1st and 2nd-year students before the official v1.0 release.
